Beginner Hosting Guide

How to Publish Your Portfolio for Free

Follow one of these three simple methods to publish your website online and submit your working link for your student activity.

1
Create a free GitHub repository

Sign in to GitHub.com, click New Repository, and name it my-portfolio (keep it Public).

2
Upload your project files

Upload index.html, styles.css, script.js, and the assets/ folder directly to the root of the repository.

3
Turn on GitHub Pages

Go to Settings > Pages. Under Build and deployment, set Source to Deploy from a branch and select main (root), then click Save.

4
Get your live link

In 1–2 minutes, your website will be live at: https://your-username.github.io/my-portfolio/!
